05 Your rights and options

You may have the right to correct or delete your data, object to or restrict how we use or share it, and withdraw consent where you previously provided consent. For example, you can turn off camera access in your mobile device settings. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before withdrawal.

Opt out of targeted advertising

We and our advertising partners may use advertising identifiers, including Android Advertising ID and Apple ID for Advertising (IDFA), and similar technologies to provide and optimize advertising.

For iOS devices

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Select Privacy.
  3. Select Advertising and enable Limit Ad Tracking (iOS 13 and older), or select Tracking and turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track (iOS 14).

For Android devices

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Select Google.
  3. Select Ads and enable Opt out of Ads Personalization.

After opting out, networks will show contextual rather than interest-based ads where possible. You may still see non-customized advertising and personalized ads from networks that obtained consent through other apps or websites.

Requests to exercise your rights should be emailed to lamkhoa.hq@gmail.com. Requests are free of charge and will be addressed as early as possible. You may also contact your local data protection authority within the European Economic Area.
